# RFM Customer Segmentation Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project features a comprehensive 2-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer behavior, track financial impact, and provide data-driven marketing strategies using RFM (Recency, Frequency, and Monetary) segmentation.

## 📊 Pages & Structure
1. **Executive Overview:** A high-level health check tracking key business metrics ($4.51M Total Revenue, 4K Total Customers) alongside customer volume treemaps and monetary value distributions.
2. **Behavioral Drill-Down:** An operational matrix table breaking down specific segment performance (Recency, Frequency, and Monetary averages) paired with an actionable marketing recommendations playbook.

## 💡 Key Analytical Insights
* **The Revenue Engines:** Champions and Loyal Customers serve as the financial backbone, generating nearly 70% ($3.10M) of the entire revenue stream.
* **The Leaking Bucket:** Over a third of the customer base resides in fading segments like "About To Sleep" (805) and "Need Attention" (557), presenting a major retention risk but a massive win-back opportunity.
